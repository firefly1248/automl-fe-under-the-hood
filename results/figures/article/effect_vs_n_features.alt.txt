Per-dataset effect against the base model versus the number of new features on a log axis, one dot per arm and dataset, for LightGBM and CatBoost. Dots cluster around zero from no new features to about two thousand; the largest swings are single datasets and appear at both few and many features. The y axis mixes AUC differences with relative loss reductions, whose scales differ by task type, so the dots are not in one common unit. Arms with no new features, or fewer than the base (down to -22), are drawn at the left edge of the log axis.
