Seven dot-and-interval panels, one dot per dataset, for the _r13 baseline and the single-model diagnostics: B-A and D-A against the _r13 GBM, then A-A_def, A_et0-A, B_et0-A_et0, B-B_def and B_et0-B, which separate the _r13 hyperparameters from the _r13 features. The _r13 hyperparameters are clearly worse than the default ones on five datasets (pol, sick, elevators, kr-vs-kp, bank-marketing) and clearly better on three; switching extra_trees off recovers part of the loss. D-A is positive on all twelve datasets, but A is a weak baseline.
