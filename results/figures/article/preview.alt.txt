Median effect of nine feature-engineering arms against plain LightGBM, in three panels (binary AUC difference, multiclass and regression relative improvement), with 95% bootstrap intervals over datasets. Almost every interval includes zero; LightAutoML pipeline on multiclass and regression lies entirely below it, AutoGluon TabPrep, AutoGluon TabPrep (raw categoricals kept) and MLJAR golden features on binary lie just above it (a quarter of an AUC point at most), and autofeat is drawn hollow on binary and multiclass because under 80% of its units finished.
