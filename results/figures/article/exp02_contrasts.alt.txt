Five dot-and-interval panels (B_def-A_def, D-A_def, D-D0, E-D0, B-B2), one dot per dataset, showing the relative loss improvement of the first AutoGluon variant over the second with a fold-level 95% interval; a dot is green when the interval is above zero, red when below, grey otherwise. D-A_def is positive on all twelve datasets; the signs differ across datasets in the other panels. The intervals cover folds only, not the variation between repeated runs of the time-limited ensembles D, D0 and E.
