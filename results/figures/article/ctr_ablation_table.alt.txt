Table of the CatBoost max_ctr_complexity ablation on the 17 datasets with a high-cardinality categorical column, with the unit of each row's effect in its label (percentage points of AUC for binary, % log loss for multiclass, % RMSE for regression). The first column of numbers gives the base arm's change when CatBoost's own categorical combinations are switched off (max_ctr_complexity=1) over default CatBoost; it is negative on most datasets, for example -2.8 AUC points on Amazon_employee_access and -25.8% RMSE on socmob. The next three columns are the arms ag_tabprep_keepcat, lama_adv and openfe; each cell lists the arm's effect over base with default CatBoost, the same effect with max_ctr_complexity=1, and their difference. Green cells mark a clearly positive difference (the fold confidence interval excludes zero), red cells a clearly negative one. The summary rows give the datasets with a positive difference by sign (11 of 17, 12 of 17 and 12 of 16), clearly positive (5, 7 and 6) and clearly negative (0, 1 and 1). fps_benchmark, where base is 537% worse without CatBoost's combinations, is an outlier left out of the rows and given in a note under the table, though counted in the summary rows; openfe has no result there because its generator failed.
