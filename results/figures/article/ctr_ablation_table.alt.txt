Table of the CatBoost max_ctr_complexity ablation on the 17 datasets with a high-cardinality categorical column. Each row is a dataset. The second column gives the relative loss improvement of the base arm when CatBoost's own categorical combinations are switched off (max_ctr_complexity=1) over default CatBoost; it is negative on most datasets (14 of 17 below zero, clearly worse on 8), for example -11.0% on Amazon_employee_access and -25.8% on socmob. The next three columns are the arms ag_tabprep_keepcat, lama_adv and openfe; each cell lists the arm's effect over base with default CatBoost, the same effect with max_ctr_complexity=1, and their difference in percentage points. Green cells mark a clearly positive difference (the fold confidence interval excludes zero), red cells a clearly negative one. The summary rows give the datasets with a positive difference by sign (13 of 17, 13 of 17 and 12 of 16), clearly positive (6, 8 and 6) and clearly negative (0, 0 and 2). openfe has no row for fps_benchmark because its generator failed there.
