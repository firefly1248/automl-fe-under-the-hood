Median effect of eight feature-engineering arms against plain LightGBM, in three panels (binary AUC difference, multiclass and regression relative improvement), with 95% bootstrap intervals over datasets. Almost every interval includes zero; lama_adv on multiclass and regression lies entirely below it, ag_tabprep, ag_tabprep_keepcat and mljar_golden on binary lie just above it (a quarter of an AUC point at most), and autofeat is drawn hollow on binary and multiclass because under 80% of its units finished.
