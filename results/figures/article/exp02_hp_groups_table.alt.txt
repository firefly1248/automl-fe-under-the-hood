Table of mean test loss over five folds on elevators, kr-vs-kp and socmob for the _r13 GBM (A), the same with extra_trees off (A_et0), A_et0 with one hyperparameter group returned to default (sampling, capacity, regularization, categorical) and the AutoGluon default GBM (A_def). Returning lambda_l1 and lambda_l2 to default brings elevators to 0.0021 and kr-vs-kp to 0.0242, the A_def level; the other groups do not on those two datasets. On socmob the categorical group gives 17.03 against 18.00 for A_def, with a fold interval that includes zero. Elevators and socmob are RMSE, kr-vs-kp is log loss; lower is better.
