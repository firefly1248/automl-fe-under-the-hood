Three dot-and-interval panels (lama-ag, h2o-ag, h2o_te-h2o), one dot per dataset, showing the relative loss improvement of LightAutoML over AutoGluon best quality, of H2O over AutoGluon, and of H2O with target encoding over H2O; a dot is green when the fold interval is above zero, red when below, grey otherwise. LightAutoML and H2O are worse than AutoGluon on most datasets. In h2o_te-h2o only okcupid-stem and Moneyball have a column that target encoding touches (both negative); the other differences are not encoding effects.
