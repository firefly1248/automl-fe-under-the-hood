Three dot-and-interval panels (lama-ag, h2o-ag, h2o_te-h2o), one dot per dataset, showing the relative loss improvement of LightAutoML over AutoGluon best quality, of H2O over AutoGluon, and of H2O with target encoding over H2O; a dot is green when the fold interval is above zero, red when below, grey otherwise. LightAutoML and H2O are worse than AutoGluon on most datasets, and target encoding in H2O is clearly worse on the two datasets where it encodes something; the other differences are not encoding effects.
