Table of the skrub TableVectorizer and AutoGluon default arms against base on the primary datasets that have at least one categorical column, one row per arm and downstream model (LightGBM, CatBoost). Columns give the number of datasets (28, or 27 for CatBoost, which skips connect-4), how many are better by the sign of the mean effect, how many have a mean effect within 1e-9 of zero (the fitted result is identical to base), and how many are clearly better or clearly worse (the bootstrap interval of the dataset's effect excludes zero). AutoGluon default, which keeps categorical columns as categories (dropping or retyping some), is close to base: on LightGBM 22 of 28 datasets are identical, 1 is better and 5 are worse (1 clearly better, 1 clearly worse); on CatBoost 15 of 27 are better, 4 identical and 8 worse (3 clearly better, 2 clearly worse). skrub TableVectorizer, which turns categoricals into numbers, on LightGBM is better on 12 of 28, clearly better on 6 and clearly worse on 10; on CatBoost better on 11 of 27, clearly better on 5 and clearly worse on 7. A few clear counts sit at the edge and move by one under other bootstrap seeds.
