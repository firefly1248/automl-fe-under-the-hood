Table of the skrub_tv and ag_default arms against base on the primary datasets that have at least one categorical column, one row per arm and downstream model (LightGBM, CatBoost). Columns give the number of datasets (28, or 27 for CatBoost, which skips connect-4), how many are better by the sign of the mean effect, and how many are clearly better or clearly worse (the bootstrap interval of the dataset's effect excludes zero). ag_default, which keeps the categorical columns, is close to base: on CatBoost it is better on 15 of 27, clearly better on 3 and clearly worse on 2, on LightGBM better on 1 of 28 with 1 clearly better and 1 clearly worse. skrub_tv, which turns categoricals into numbers, on LightGBM is better on 12 of 28, clearly better on 6 and clearly worse on 10; on CatBoost better on 11 of 27, clearly better on 5 and clearly worse on 7.
