# Article

One Medium article, "What automated feature engineering really does under the hood". Every number in it is a
claim id in `results/claims.yaml`; every image is a PNG of at least 1400 px in `results/figures/article/` with a
hand-written `<name>.alt.txt` next to it (`make article` writes the PNGs and the data tables they render, as the text of the cells for the table images;
the figure claims hash those tables). Section 4 carries `preview.png` (1500 x 680 px, white margins) in the text.

| section | topic | experiments | claim ids | images (`results/figures/article/`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction: what the tools promise, what is compared | - | - | - |
| 2 | What the tools do under the hood; AutoGluon | exp01, exp02, probes of exp03, exp05 and exp06 | `s2_*` | `transform_map`, `exp02_contrasts`, `exp02_single_model`, `exp02_contrasts_table`, `exp02_single_model_table`, `exp02_hp_groups_table` |
| 3 | LightAutoML | exp03 | `s3_*` | - |
| 4 | Open benchmark: do the generated features help | exp04 | `s4_*` | `preview`, `median_effect_lgbm`, `median_effect_catboost`, `win_tie_loss_lgbm`, `win_tie_loss_catboost`, `effect_vs_gen_time`, `effect_vs_n_features`, `coverage_table` |
| 5 | Leakage checks and planted signals | exp05 | `s5_*` | `permuted_labels`, `planted_signals` |
| 6 | AutoML end to end | exp06 | `s6_*` | `exp06_contrasts`, `exp06_contrasts_table`, `exp06_lama_diag_table` |

Results for the contrasts of sections 2 and 6 are given as "N better of M datasets" (the main claim) with the
per-dataset table beside it; the median over datasets is a secondary claim (`role: secondary`), because four
datasets per task type, or two for multiclass in section 6, are too few for a median to carry a conclusion.

Two inputs are not regenerated by `make`: `results/tidy/transform_map.csv` and
`transform_map_evidence.csv` are the source of truth for the map (each cell cites exp01, exp03 or exp05 outputs, the
probe tables `exp03_dates_text.csv` and `exp06_h2o_probe.csv`, or source lines; they were assembled once and are edited by hand);
`make article` redraws `transform_map.png` from them. `s2_fig_transform_map` hashes the map table, and the `s2_map_*`
claims count its statuses, which are two values (run output, source code) plus not confirmed; unverified parts of
a cell are in the `note` column of the evidence table. In the map the `h2o` column covers the default run and the run with
target encoding together, and some `lama` cells come from the `lama_adv` arm of exp05.

In the contrast tables `better (sign)` counts datasets whose mean effect is above zero; `clearly better` and
`clearly worse` count datasets whose fold CI excludes zero. Dots in the contrast figures use the same colours.
